# SAS trustworthy AI lifecycle

For an agent helping model, risk, data and engineering owners prepare governance evidence and monitoring plans, or independently review supplied evidence. In a review, inspect and disposition owner-defined criteria, results and decisions; return gaps to the accountable operational or model owner rather than designing tests, selecting thresholds or executing release work. For plan preparation, record proposed evidence gates, monitoring responsibilities and unresolved owner decisions without inventing policy or approval. This skill supplies procedure, not approval, certification, credentials or permission to deploy, schedule monitoring, access sensitive data or change a workflow. It works without SAS software for document preparation.

## Workflow

1. **Bind the assignment.** Identify the requested artifact and review stage, model/project or release ID and revision, intended use, affected people, deployment destination, supplied evidence and permitted effects. Separate model validation from validation of the full decision flow. A rules-only change does not automatically need model training or champion/challenger selection; mark model-only items not applicable with a reason, and identify the accountable product owner for outstanding execution work.
2. **Record versions and owners.** For document-only work, record model/data/policy revisions and evidence dates. For SAS integration, additionally record the exact product, Viya family, platform release/cadence/patches, Model Manager/Workflow Manager availability and workflow definition version where relevant. Unknown versions block compatibility claims, not an offline review. Assign accountable people or teams for model ownership, model development, deployment, risk, data and domain expertise; one person can hold multiple roles, but do not invent assignments or approvals.
3. **Build the evidence record.** Read [lifecycle evidence templates](references/lifecycle-evidence.md). Populate only the requested sections using supplied or authorized evidence. Bind each applicable owner-defined acceptance criterion to its revision-specific result, locator and date; record it as evidenced, missing, failed, or accepted exception. Use not applicable only with a reason. An absent criterion, unrun check or asserted result without supporting evidence is missing, not passed or a demonstrated failure. For a recorded exception retain the actual gap/failed result, authorized acceptor, rationale, conditions and expiry/review date. Reference protected records rather than copying PII, credentials or sensitive transaction payloads into the report.
4. **Apply the domain branch.** When a model informs fraud detection or decisions, or the review covers fraud rules, alert feedback or rule-impact evidence without a model change, read [fraud review](references/fraud-review.md) before dispositioning evidence. For rules/alerts, apply its [release and feedback governance procedure](references/fraud-review.md#review-rule-release-and-alert-feedback-evidence) as well as the relevant population/metric sections. These are independently authored review procedures, not SAS-provided fraud thresholds. Request missing acceptance criteria, thresholds or harm/policy decisions from their named owners.
5. **Separate readiness from execution.** Assess supplied test, deployment, monitoring and approval records against their recorded scope and criteria. Return each gap or failed criterion to its accountable owner with the evidence/decision needed to close it; operational owners retain test design, deployment planning, execution and remediation. A model-owner approval does not prove deployment validation or approve a whole Fraud Decisioning release. When asked to use the SAS-delivered governance workflow, read [Model Manager workflow boundaries](references/model-manager-workflow.md) before proposing an import or activation. Record unavailable prerequisites; do not install products to complete a checklist.
6. **Deliver the requested artifact.** Return scope/revisions, evidence table, bounded readiness disposition, outstanding owner actions and recorded approvals, and monitoring/escalation evidence or a proposed plan where requested. Distinguish proposed plans, supplied plans, observed results, missing evidence and demonstrated failures; leave absent threshold, cadence or response decisions with their named owners. For a reported deployment, inspect evidence of the selected revision and business-relevant outputs against owner-approved sample cases; request any absent runtime evidence rather than executing the validation as part of review. State deployment and live monitoring unverified when unsupported. The agent can recommend, but only the designated organization owners can accept risk and approve release.
