No upstream prose, code, datasets, illustrations or assets are copied or adapted into it; examples are original and synthetic. Consultation does not confer permission to execute, obtain data or approve a decision. No new license grant is made for this package or for upstream material. Preserve upstream rights and notices if separately obtaining or redistributing their materials; their licenses do not automatically cover this package.

All sources below were actually consulted on **2026-10-28**. Dates/editions identify the consulted evidence, not a deployment-compatibility or future-content guarantee. This package does not prescribe version-specific software APIs or assume any software is installed.

## Authorship and boundaries

- The ordered review workflow, permissions, population/grain/lineage binding, join-multiplication checks, temporal-leakage and selection questions, severity scheme, coverage statuses and findings format are original operational synthesis. Sources support the scoped principles above, not every checklist item or a guarantee of statistical validity.
- Negative revenue, growth above 100%, incomplete observations and nonzero axes are evaluated against metric definitions and intended interpretation; these sources are not asserted to impose universal rejection thresholds.
- The 100-user/10-user weighted-mean fixture and before/after causal-claim fixture are synthetic package examples, not reproduced upstream datasets. Their supplied summaries cannot establish source completeness, causal effect or actual runtime verification.
- Anthropic's `validate-data` or other third-party skills were not consulted or copied for this package; no provenance prefix or adaptation claim is attached.
- Official citations are methodological evidence. Unknown inputs, absent permissions and uninspected data remain unknown, not “unsupported software” or proof of erroneous results. Readiness is always relative to the inspected evidence and named use.
