The SAS programming and model-development packages implement **version-neutral procedure, version-bound technical claims**. Their local references distinguish documentation applicability, publication/research dates, observed target capability and exercised behavior. A source pin is not a compatibility range; adjacent or unknown releases remain unverified until evidence resolves the affected operation.
